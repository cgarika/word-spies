HOW TO PLAY — 30 SECONDS
1. Two teams. The 25 words belong secretly to Red, Blue, bystanders — and one 💀 trap.
2. Each team has one 🗝️ spymaster who can see the secret colors. Everyone else is a 🕵️ guesser and sees plain words.
3. On your team's turn, your spymaster sends one word + a number — like OCEAN · 2 means "two of our words relate to OCEAN".
4. Guessers talk it out (use voice!) and tap words. Your color = keep going. Wrong color or bystander = turn over.
5. Find all your team's words first to win. Tap the 💀 and you lose instantly.
